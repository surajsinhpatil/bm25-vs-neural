# How to judge (Group 10)

1. **Unzip first**, then double-click `judge_<YourName>.html` so it opens in Chrome, Edge or
   Firefox. Do not grade inside a zip preview, an editor preview or the Kaggle/GitHub file
   viewer. Those do not run the page's script, so they show every item as a read-only list
   with an orange warning at the top.
2. Start with `practice_<YourName>.html` (about 30 items) to calibrate. Practice grades are
   never used. Compare your practice grades with the others' before you start the real page.
3. Each item shows the query, its description, **what counts as relevant**, and the paper's
   title and abstract. For time-constraint queries it also shows the paper's first-version
   date. Grade with the buttons or the keys `0`–`3`:
   * 0: not relevant
   * 1: on the topic, but does not answer the query or breaks a condition
     (an exclusion, or the date limit)
   * 2: relevant
   * 3: highly relevant (the paper is mainly about exactly this)
4. Grades are saved in your browser as you go. Always reopen the page from the same file in
   the same browser. Do not use a private window.
5. Click **Export judgments** at least once per session. It downloads
   `judgments_<YourName>_main.json`. Send that file (not the HTML page) to the person who
   commits to `judging/exports/`.
6. To continue on another computer or browser, open the page there and use **Import** with
   your latest exported file.
7. Judge **Phase 1** items first (they come first on the page). Do not discuss specific
   documents with your partner annotator until both of you have exported. Agreement (κ) is
   computed on independent grades.
